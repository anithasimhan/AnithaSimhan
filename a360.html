Employee Platform · Case Study
Associate 360 — Profile Management

A profile management platform redesigned to bring everything an associate needs into one place — cleaner, faster, and easier to navigate for both Associates and Managers at Cognizant.

Problem
The platform was slow, cluttered, and hard to navigate. Profile data was scattered, notifications for incomplete information were missing, and the FAQ was nearly impossible to find.
My Role
End-to-end Experience Designer — research, personas, IA review, UI redesign, usability testing, and collaboration with developers and stakeholders.
Approach
8 one-on-one moderated interviews with Associates and Managers. Created personas for both user types. Redesigned into a single consolidated view with clear notifications and improved mobile experience.
Solution
Cleaner, more organised experience with better profile visibility. Friendlier navigation and positive feedback on mobile — validated across 28 remote usability sessions.

UI Screens
UI Screens

Clean. Structured.
Easy to navigate.

The final UI screens bring together a simplified, consolidated view — ensuring users can see everything they need without excessive scrolling or complex navigation paths.

Key Screens

The key screens include Profile Certification with About Me, Address and Contact sections; a simple navigation covering Home, Profile, Resume Builder, Beneficiary, Utilization, FAQs and Settings; and details such as Emergency Contact, National ID (PAN + Aadhar) and Manager Details. The application has many more sections beyond these, which follow the same clean and consistent layout.
