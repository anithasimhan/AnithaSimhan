Career Platform · Case Study
C-Ahead — Opportunity Search

Cognizant's internal career platform redesigned to serve two very different users — Associates searching for opportunities and Hiring Managers allocating candidates — both efficiently, in one system.

Problem
Associates couldn't find relevant opportunities due to unclear navigation and limited filters. Hiring Managers struggled to search, allocate, and propose candidates. The experience was inconsistent and required heavy manual effort.
My Role
End-to-end Experience Designer — research, sprint facilitation, proto-personas, wireframing, UI design, and multi-round usability validation for both user types.
Approach
15 interviews across regions, proto-personas for both user types, and a 7-day design sprint with cross-functional teams. Redesigned search, navigation, opportunity discovery, and candidate allocation workflows.
Solution
Redesigned search, filters, and allocation workflows for both Associates and Hiring Managers. Clearer navigation, personalised suggestions, and structured job proposals — reducing manual effort and improving talent visibility across Cognizant.
01 — Discover (Research)

Interview planning
& execution.

By planning and conducting interviews with Associates and Hiring Managers, I gathered insights into user needs, motivations, challenges, and existing workflow pain points. This helped build a strong understanding of the current experience and shaped the foundation for all later design decisions.

  • Users rely heavily on manual workarounds to compensate for missing platform features — spreadsheets used alongside the platform for tracking.
  • Role discovery is fragmented; Associates cannot easily surface opportunities relevant to their skills or business line.
  • Hiring Managers face information overload with too many profiles and insufficient filtering or sorting controls.
  • Navigation structure is misaligned with the actual mental model of both user types, causing repeated back-and-forth actions.
  • Lack of onboarding guidance leaves new Associates unsure about where to start or which section leads to the correct task.

02 — Define (Understanding Users)

Proto Persona
Development.

Using early research insights, I developed proto-personas that captured user goals, behaviors, frustrations, and expectations. These helped align the team around who we were designing for and guided problem-framing and initial concept exploration.

G

Age 29 · Canada · 5+ Years Experience
Recently joined Cognizant — enthusiastic but unfamiliar with internal applications and policies.
Goals
  • Understand the internal job ecosystem quickly
  • Explore new roles and opportunities regularly

  • Navigation feels unclear, causing repeated back-and-forth actions
  • Lacks confidence due to limited familiarity with internal processes

Age 45 · Canada · 15+ Years Experience
Checks C-Ahead occasionally — mainly during bench periods when actively looking for new assignments.
Goals
  • Quickly understand which roles match their skillset
  • Complete tasks with minimal navigation and clear instructions
  • Avoid unnecessary complexity in the system
Pain Points
  • Platform feels confusing and overwhelming due to multiple sections
  • Not confident with technology, causing difficulty navigating features
  • Too much information creates overload; struggles to identify what matters
03 — Validation & Refinement

Usability Testing
in two rounds.

By observing users completing real tasks in the prototype, I identified usability issues, refined information flow, clarified interactions, and ensured the design was simple and efficient. This validation confirmed which solutions worked well and what required improvement.
