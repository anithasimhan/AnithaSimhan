Problem Statement

Why did the app
need a redesign?

The original Bima Paathshala app had several critical issues preventing rural users from fully engaging with the product.

ISSUE 01

Confusing onboarding with too many manual steps — language selection, data entry, and navigation were overwhelming for first-time users.

ISSUE 02

Deep, complex navigation — important features like Courses and Certificates were buried inside the hamburger menu, hard to discover.

ISSUE 03

No personalization — all users saw the same content regardless of their interests or learning goals.

ISSUE 04

Horizontal scroll on the home screen felt unnatural on mobile — especially for users new to smartphones.

ISSUE 05

No global search — users had to navigate deep into each section separately to find content.

ISSUE 06

Referral feature was invisible and unmotivating — a huge missed opportunity for organic growth.

🎯

Design Goal: Redesign Bima Paathshala to make insurance learning simple, accessible, and engaging for rural users — while measurably increasing referrals, course completions, and retention.

SWOT Analysis

Honest evaluation of the product before redesigning it.

💪 Strengths

  • Free video content for all users
  • Referral bonus system
  • Content made for rural users
  • Professional video instructions

⚠️ Weaknesses

  • Confusing onboarding experience
  • Limited video progress tracking
  • Navigation needs improvement
  • Profile and notifications are limited

🚀 Opportunities

  • Offline video downloads for rural users
  • Language preference settings
  • Stronger referral triggers
  • Personalized course suggestions

🔻 Threats

  • Poor navigation may cause user drop-off
  • Weak referral system limits growth
  • Competitors offer better experience
  • Links to social media and poor UX

Heuristic Evaluation

Evaluated the existing app against Jakob Nielsen's 10 Usability Principles.

ISSUE 01 · User Control & Freedom
Manual Language Selection

The app required users to manually select a language during onboarding — adding friction for low-literacy rural users.

✦ Auto-detect language from phone settings
ISSUE 02 · User Control & Freedom
Limited Signup Channels

Only phone number signup was available — creating unnecessary friction when faster alternatives exist.

✦ Add Facebook & Gmail signup options
ISSUE 03 · Efficiency of Use
No Auto-fill for Personal Details

Users signing up via social accounts still had to manually enter all personal details — causing drop-off.

✦ Auto-fill details from social login
ISSUE 04 · Aesthetic & Minimalist Design
Horizontal Scroll on Home Screen

The home screen used horizontal scrolling which felt unnatural on mobile and caused navigation confusion.

✦ Change to vertical scroll

User Survey

45%

78%

78% said Yes — validating the importance of the referral and social sharing features in our design.

Do you prefer Website or App?

82%

82% preferred Mobile App — confirming our mobile-first design approach was correct.

Final Screens

The redesigned
experience, live.

Four key screens showing the redesigned Bima Paathshala in action — from first-time onboarding through daily use, built for rural, mobile-first users across India.

Screen 01
Language Selection

10 regional languages as large, tappable buttons. Onboarding begins in the user's own language — removing the literacy barrier from screen one.

Screen 02
Home — Referral & Progress

Referral banner front and centre. Course Progress motivates continued learning. Vertical scroll replaces the confusing horizontal layout from v1.

Screen 03
Navigation Drawer

User profile at top, then My Course, My Wallet, Leader Board, App Feedback, My Profile, Logout — all key paths clearly surfaced and labelled.

Screen 04
Course Feedback Modal

5-star rating with comments field — collecting satisfaction data after content engagement, not intrusively mid-session.

Thank You
