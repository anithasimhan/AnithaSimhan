Access Platform · Case Study
IDM — Identity & Access Management

IDM (Identity and Access Management) is a role-based enterprise system used by teams at NTB to securely review, approve, and revoke user access across applications and entitlements.

Problem
Users struggled to review and approve access. Inconsistent screens, information overload, and confusing navigation made critical workflows hard to complete efficiently.
My Role
End-to-end Experience Designer — user research, information architecture, wireframes, visual design, and usability testing. Sole designer on the project.
Approach
10 moderated user interviews, personas, and journey maps. Redesigned IA around Certification, Verification, and Rule Management. Introduced a dashboard-first experience with clearer CTAs and confirmation flows.
Outcome
Faster task completion, fewer approval errors, and better workflow discoverability. Users felt more confident and in control — validated with 15 usability testers.

Understanding the Users

To design the right solution, I first needed to understand who was using the system and where they were struggling. I conducted one on one moderated interviews with 10 users across two groups — ABO and ATO — with an equal mix of both genders

Moderated Sessions

Key Pain Points Discovered

🗺️

Hard to Navigate

Users couldn't easily find key workflows and were forced to click through multiple screens just to reach their starting point.

🔀

Inconsistent Screens

Pages looked and behaved differently, making the system feel unpredictable. Users couldn't build confidence using the product.

📊

Information Overload

Too much data appeared at once without clear priority. Users didn't know what to focus on, and important information was buried.

❓

Lack of Guidance & Feedback

After completing an action, users had no confirmation it worked. No next step guidance, leaving users unsure whether to continue or stop.

Defining Who We're Designing For

Based on the research insights, I created two user personas that represented the core user groups — capturing their goals, frustrations, and daily realities within the IDM system.

👩
Peace Jason
Age 32 · Application Technology Owner (ATO)

Peace is comfortable with technology and handles recurring certification tasks. She needs fast workflows, quick navigation, and real-time status updates.

Goals
  • Complete reviews efficiently and quickly
  • Filter and locate specific access details fast
  • Understand what's pending at a glance
  • Reduce repeated manual effort
Frustrations
  • Certification screens feel cluttered and slow
  • Too many clicks to complete a single review
  • No real-time updates or clear status visibility
👨
Dolapo Samuel
Age 51 · Application Business Owner (ABO)

Sam has 25+ years of experience but doesn't use digital systems often. He prefers simple, predictable workflows and struggles when screens feel crowded.

Goals
  • Review and approve access quickly without mistakes
  • Find all pending tasks in one central place
  • Get clear feedback after every action
Frustrations
  • Navigation is confusing and unpredictable
  • Too many screens and options at once
  • Flows are not guided step-by-step

Prototype / Final Screens

Designed Screens

The final visual designs captured improved navigation, simplified layouts, and a complete interface experience — all built on the client's design system for consistency.

Screen 01
Dashboard — Welcome & Action ItemsRedesigned landing page showing personalised welcome, real-time task counters (270 Access Requests, 64 Verifications, 3220 Certifications) and Quick Links navigation — giving users an instant overview the moment they log in.
Screen 02
Certification Review — Function Roles & EntitlementsStructured review screen with KPI summary bar, clearly grouped Function Roles and Applications sections, inline approve/reject decision controls and collapsible detail rows — resolving the original information overload users described as "overwhelming."
Screen 03
Verifications Dashboard — Certification ListClean, structured list showing all verification types (Technology Owner, Business Owner, Description Quality, Function Role) with pending counts, completion status, due dates and direct Start Review actions — replacing the original buried, hard-to-find workflow.
5
Test

Usability Testing & Validation

After completing the redesign, I validated the solution with 15 end users across ABO and ATO groups through moderated usability testing. I gave users specific tasks to complete and observed where they succeeded, hesitated, or struggled.

✅

Task Success Rate - 100%

All 15 users successfully completed their assigned tasks across certification, verification, and role management workflows.

⏱

Time on Task - 4to5 min

Users completed key workflows significantly faster than with the original interface — fewer clicks, shorter paths.

📉

Error Rate - 75% reduction

Mistakes dropped noticeably. Users moved through tasks with confidence and rarely took wrong turns.

😊

Satisfaction Score - positive

Post-session feedback was overwhelmingly positive — users described the redesign as cleaner, more guided, and easier to control.
