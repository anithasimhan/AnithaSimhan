Overview

One platform.
Many experiences.

Moment was created to become the central design platform for DX Strategy & Design.

Rather than building from scratch for every initiative, teams can leverage shared design foundations, reusable components, templates, and domain-based page patterns to accelerate product creation while maintaining consistency.

Vision

Beyond a component library.

The goal wasn't to create another UI kit.

The vision was to build a design platform that enables teams to move faster from idea to experience by providing reusable frameworks that scale across products, domains, and future AI-powered workflows.
